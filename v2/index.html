The rooms

Slow down, breathe and enjoy.

Ombak Damai is a roomstay: four rooms in a row along a shaded veranda, each with its own front door. Inside, the walls are exposed brick, the beds are made up in plain white, and the doors are painted a soft yellow.

Take one room for a quiet weekend, or all four and have the whole place to yourselves.

  • Two double beds in every room
  • Private bathroom in every room
  • Air-conditioning and fan
  • Kettle, cups and drinking water
  • Fresh towels
  • Shared kitchen and living area
  • Splash pool in the courtyard
  • BBQ and bonfire area
  • Parking inside the compound
  • Sleeps up to 16 across four rooms

The whole coast

Slow days on the Setiu coast.

10 km South China Sea

  1. Rhu Sepuluh, where some forty celup tepung stalls line the beach road. The sea is a kilometre away.

  2. Six minutes up the road. Miles of sand, coconut palms and fishing villages, with boats landing the morning catch and Redang and Bidong on the horizon.

  3. Thirteen minutes north, where Terrapuri has restored traditional Terengganu timber houses and painted terrapin hatchlings are released into the river.

  4. Lagoons, mangroves and river channels behind the coast. Boats go out at sunset for the river, and again after dark for the fireflies.

  5. Sixteen minutes down the coast road. The boats for Redang, Lang Tengah and Bidong leave from here.

  6. Redang, Lang Tengah and Bidong: turtles, sandbanks and clear water, a boat ride from Merang. About March to October.

  7. Up the coast, for the boats to the Perhentian islands.

  8. Kuala Terengganu airportAbout 50 minutes
  9. Kuala Lumpur by roadAbout five and a half hours

Good to know

The small print, kept short.

Booking and deposit

Send your request on WhatsApp and we confirm the rooms. A 50% deposit by bank transfer holds your dates, and the balance is paid on arrival. Nothing is charged online.

One room or the whole place

Book a single room and you share the kitchen, pavilion and pool with other guests. Book all four and the roomstay is yours alone, with room for 16.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 3.00 pm, check-out by 12.00 pm. Tell us if you are arriving early or leaving late and we will do what we can.

Monsoon season

From about November to February the sea is rough and the island boats stop. It is also when the coast is at its quietest, with big skies and big waves.

Families and groups

Children are welcome, and the splash pool is theirs. Please keep an eye on little ones around the water and the fire.

Trips and extras

Island trips, fishing boats, and BBQ and bonfire nights are arranged on request. Tick what you want when you book and we will quote on WhatsApp.

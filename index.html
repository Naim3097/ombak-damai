Slow down, breathe and enjoy.

No. 02 · Days out

Every getaway deserves a little adventure.

Snorkel with sea turtles

Boats leave from Merang jetty, 16 minutes down the coast road, for a day among the islands off Redang. Slip into clear water where the turtles come up to feed, wade the shallow sandbank, and be back in time for dinner by the fire.

  • A full day on the water
  • Boats from Merang jetty
  • About March to October, when the sea allows

Go fishing with the locals

Fishing rods wait along the fence. Boats head out through the river mouth at first light and come back with a catch like this one. On this coast the squid-jigging season runs from about April to September.

  • River and sea trips by boat
  • Bring your catch back for the grill
  • Ask us about dates and boats

Or stay in and do very little

The courtyard is sand underfoot, with a splash pool for the children, a shaded pavilion for long lunches, and a shared kitchen to cook in. Nobody will mind if you never leave it.

  • Splash pool and sand courtyard
  • Shaded pavilion and veranda
  • Shared kitchen and living area

Good to know. Island trips pause for the monsoon, from about November to February, when the sea is rough. The rooms, the bonfire and the celup tepung stalls carry on all year.

No. 04 · Around Penarik

Slow days on the Setiu coast.

01

Celup tepung at Rhu Sepuluh

Our own neighbourhood is famous for it: seafood dipped in batter and fried to order at some forty stalls along the beach road. In June 2026 they set a national record here with a kilometre-long plate of battered prawns.

02

Pantai Penarik

Six minutes up the road. Miles of sand, coconut palms and fishing villages, with boats landing the morning catch and Redang and Bidong on the horizon.

03

Setiu Wetlands

Lagoons, mangroves and river channels behind the coast. Boats go out at sunset for the river, and again after dark for the fireflies.

04

Kampung Mangkuk

Thirteen minutes north, where Terrapuri has restored traditional Terengganu timber houses and painted terrapin hatchlings are released into the river.

05

The islands

Merang jetty is 16 minutes away for boats to Redang, Lang Tengah and Bidong. Kuala Besut, for the Perhentians, is under an hour.

No. 07 · Good to know

The small print, kept short.

Booking and deposit

Send your request on WhatsApp and we confirm the rooms. A 50% deposit by bank transfer holds your dates, and the balance is paid on arrival. Nothing is charged online.

One room or the whole place

Book a single room and you share the kitchen, pavilion and pool with other guests. Book all four and the roomstay is yours alone, with room for 16.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 3.00 pm, check-out by 12.00 pm. Tell us if you are arriving early or leaving late and we will do what we can.

Monsoon season

From about November to February the sea is rough and the island boats stop. It is also when the coast is at its quietest, with big skies and big waves.

Families and groups

Children are welcome, and the splash pool is theirs. Please keep an eye on little ones around the water and the fire.

Trips and extras

Island trips, fishing boats, and BBQ and bonfire nights are arranged on request. Tick what you want when you book and we will quote on WhatsApp.

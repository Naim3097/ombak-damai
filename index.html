01, The house

Built the kampung way.

Timber and brick as Terengganu builds them. Raised floors, wide eaves, windows set to catch the sea breeze. Inside it stays simple and cared for: rattan and cotton, cool floors, beds made up in plain white.

The house sleeps eight across three air-conditioned bedrooms, with the veranda holding the long afternoons and the kitchen ready for proper cooking.

The coast at Penarik, five minutes from the door

02, Penarik

Slow days, salt air.

01

Pantai Penarik

Five minutes on foot. A long ribbon of sand under the rhu trees, with fishing boats coming in before breakfast and nobody counting the hours.

02

Setiu Wetlands

Lagoons and river channels a short drive north. Boats go out in the evening when the fireflies start along the banks.

03

Kampung Mangkuk

The next village along the coast, known for its restored century-old Terengganu timber houses and quiet river mouth.

04

The islands

Merang jetty is about twenty minutes away for boats to Redang. Kuala Besut, the gateway to the Perhentians, is under an hour.

05, Good to know

The small print, kept short.

Booking and deposit

A 50% deposit confirms your dates, settled by bank transfer over WhatsApp. The balance is paid on arrival. Nothing is charged online.

One group at a time

The whole house is yours for the stay. We never let rooms separately, and we never double up groups.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 3.00 pm, check-out by 12.00 pm. Early arrivals and late departures are usually fine midweek, just ask.

House rules

The house is alcohol free, and smoke free indoors. No pets, please. Shoes stay at the door, kampung style.

Families

Children are welcome. A baby cot and extra floor mattresses are available on request at no charge.

Quiet hours

The kampung sleeps early. We ask for quiet outdoors after 11.00 pm, out of respect for the neighbours.
